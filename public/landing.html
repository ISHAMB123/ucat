The gap

A question bank shows your score. It rarely trains what moves it.

Two things decide a UCAT mark: your pace against the clock, and whether you understand the technique behind an answer. Volume alone trains neither.

Pace

The clock is the exam

Most marks are lost to timing, not difficulty. Every drill runs on the real per-section clock, so pace is something you build, not discover on the day.

Technique

Why beats what

Each answer comes with the reasoning and the exact trap you fell for, so the next question of its kind is easier, not just newer.

Focus

Your mistakes, chased

The questions that beat you come back on a spaced schedule until they stop beating you. Clearing that list is worth more than any fresh set.

Straight answers

The questions worth asking.

Is Tempo affiliated with the UCAT?

No. It is an independent study tool, not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the UCAT Consortium, Pearson VUE or UCAS. UCAT is a trademark of its owner, used here only to describe what this app prepares you for. All content is original.

Does it replace my question bank?

No, and it is not sold as one. Banks give you volume. Tempo builds the speed, technique and judgement underneath a score, and tells you why each answer was wrong. It runs alongside whichever bank you already use.

Do you promise a score?

No. Anyone promising a UCAT score is guessing. Tempo trains the skills honestly and shows you your progress; the result on the day is yours.

What happens to my data?

Marking runs on your device, so your practice stays with you. You can export everything or delete it at any time.

Is there an app to download?

No. Tempo runs in your browser on any device, so there is nothing to install and nothing to update. Open the page, sign in and start.

I am applying in Australia or New Zealand.

There is a section for you. Tempo covers the UCAT as sat for UK, Australian and New Zealand schools.
